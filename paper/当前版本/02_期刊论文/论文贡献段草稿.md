## 一、写作前提：三个必须守住的边界

在写贡献之前，先明确**不能说什么**，否则整段会崩。

| ❌ 不能写 | 原因 |
|---|---|
| 首次提出长度前缀 / 分帧机制 | 长度前缀是常识（UDP、TCP、TS、HTTP chunked 都有） |
| 首次研究容器误码敏感性 | Iqbal & Zepernick 2019 已量化 TS 包内敏感度 |
| 我们发现外码真实功能是分帧 | "发现"是叙事，不是贡献；要写成"机制 + 准则" |
| 我们优化了平台使其能跑视频 | 平台官方示例已支持视频推流；本文做的是实时业务链路 |

| ✅ 可以说 | 依据 |
|---|---|
| 首次在同一物理误码流下横向对比四种容器/解码器栈并给出 γ | 检索未见同类 |
| 首次刻画"边界信息位置"与"失步"的因果关系（四模式消融） | 检索未见 |
| 提出"边界格子应与传输块对齐"的设计准则 | 本文结论 |
| 构建了实时视频业务试验床并给出时延分解方法 | 自建，代码可查 |

## 二、中文贡献段（正文用）

> 本文的贡献可概括为**一条设计准则与四种验证实现**。
>
> **（1）提出"边界信息位置决定失效模式"这一分析视角。** 在变长载荷经定长块传输的链路上，载荷边界必须借助显式信息确定。本文将边界信息的承载方式归纳为三类：算术约定（定长）、数据内嵌（长度字段）、标记搜索（分隔符），并指出其关键区别在于**边界信息是否存在于可能被误码破坏的数据之中**。据此给出设计准则：**边界格子应与传输块对齐，或具备独立于信道码的可验证性**。
>
> **（2）定位了分帧元数据的单点故障，并给出因果证据。** 在 RS–LDPC 级联链路上，用于切分 UDP 载荷的 2 字节长度字段位于外码码字内部，其保护完全依赖外码纠错。本文通过四种分帧方式的对照实验表明：无任何边界约定时，接收端无法定界，业务可用率恒为零且与误码率无关（M0）；采用裸长度字段时，长度字段被破坏导致解析起点偏移，且偏移沿包序列累积，形成流级失步（M1）【待补：失步率 vs p】；改用定长分片后，边界由传输块序号直接确定，失步事件消失（M3）【待补：实测失步次数】。进一步通过人为翻转长度字段 1/2/3 比特，将该差异由统计关联推进为因果结论【待补：翻转实验数据】。
>
> **（3）证明"检出不足以保证恢复"，并给出两种并列的应对方案。** 常见的直觉是在长度字段上附加校验，检出错误即丢弃该块。实验表明该做法【待补：是否反而劣于 M1】——因为丢弃会在字节流中制造空洞，而空洞对帧间预测解码器的影响不小于错位（M2a）。真正有效的做法有两条：其一，检出失败后**放弃长度字段的权威性**，退回基于起始码的重新同步，代价是局部重同步开销（M2b）；其二，**取消长度字段**，将载荷按传输块定长切分，代价是丧失报文边界、仅适用于自定界载荷（M3）。二者并非新旧关系，而是"保留报文边界的通用方案"与"彻底消除失步的专用方案"之间的取舍。
>
> **（4）给出了同一误码流下容器与解码器的量化放大，并分解其成因。** 在关闭外码的压力工况下，四种传输栈的业务可用率与等效放大因子 γ 相差约两个数量级：MPEG-TS + 硬件解码路径约 500×，裸流 + 容错解码器约 4×【待补：p 的实测值与 95% 置信区间】。本文将 γ 分解为静态头部暴露 $\gamma_{\mathrm{header}}$ 与解码器状态机 $\gamma_{\mathrm{state}}$ 两个因子，指出后者才是跨栈差异的主因：TS 接收端需依靠同步字节重建 188 字节网格，同步失守后进入重扫描，而硬件解码路径进一步将解码节奏耦合至 PCR 时钟，导致长时间冻结而非短暂降质。该结论补充了既有工作对"包内字节敏感度"的分析，把问题从"封装内部该保护哪一段"推进到"该不该采用该封装"。
>
> **（5）构建了实时视频业务试验床并给出时延分解方法。** 现有 ISAC 平台的视频功能停留在文件推流与播放器回放层面，未针对实时业务的时延约束做设计。本文打通"摄像头采集 → H.264 编码 → UDP → OFDM-ISAC → 解码 → 显示"的完整实时链路，建立端到端时延的测量与分解方法，并识别出队列正反馈环路：深队列下解码器停顿会经由接收缓冲、解调阻塞反向传导，使时延无界累积。将四级队列深度由 32 帧降至 4–8 帧并以非阻塞入队替代阻塞写入后，时延漂移率由【待补】降至【待补】ms/min，其中解码环节占约 80%，射频链路仅贡献数毫秒。

## 三、英文版（投稿用）

> This paper contributes **one design principle and four validating implementations**.
>
> **(1) A framing-information-placement perspective.** When variable-length payloads traverse a fixed-block link, payload boundaries must be established by explicit information. We classify boundary carriage into three families — arithmetic convention (fixed length), in-band metadata (length field), and marker search (delimiter) — and show that their decisive difference is **whether the boundary information resides in data that bit errors can corrupt**. This yields a design principle: **boundary grids should align with transport blocks, or carry verifiability independent of the channel code.**
>
> **(2) Localization of the framing metadata as a single point of failure, with causal evidence.** In the RS–LDPC concatenated link, the two-byte length field that delimits UDP payloads resides inside the outer codeword, so its protection depends entirely on outer-code correction. Four framing variants show that: with no boundary convention, the receiver cannot delimit at all and service availability is identically zero irrespective of the error rate (M0); with a bare length field, corruption of that field offsets the parse origin, and the offset accumulates along the packet sequence to produce stream-level desynchronization (M1) 【TBD: loss-of-sync rate vs p】; with fixed-size chunking, boundaries follow from the transport block index and desynchronization disappears (M3) 【TBD: measured desync count】. Deliberately flipping 1/2/3 bits of the length field further elevates the effect from statistical association to causation 【TBD: flip experiment】.
>
> **(3) Detection alone does not guarantee recovery, and two parallel remedies.** A natural reflex is to append a checksum to the length field and discard the block on failure. Measurements show this 【TBD: whether worse than M1】, because discarding punctures the byte stream, and a punctured stream harms an inter-frame decoder no less than a misaligned one (M2a). Two remedies are effective: after a failed check, relinquish the authority of the length field and fall back to start-code resynchronization (M2b); or remove the length field entirely and chunk the payload by transport block (M3). These are not successive versions but a trade-off between a general scheme that preserves message boundaries and a specialized one that eliminates desynchronization outright.
>
> **(4) Quantified container and decoder amplification, with attribution.** Under an outer-code-disabled stress condition, the four transport stacks differ by roughly two orders of magnitude in service availability and in the equivalent amplification factor γ: MPEG-TS with a hardware decode path reaches about 500×, whereas a bare Annex B stream with a fault-tolerant decoder stays near 4× 【TBD: measured p with 95% CI】. We factor γ into a static header-exposure term γ_header and a decoder-state-machine term γ_state, and identify the latter as the dominant cross-stack driver: a TS receiver must rebuild its 188-byte grid from sync bytes, and loss of that grid triggers rescan, while the hardware decode path further couples decode pacing to the PCR clock, producing prolonged freeze rather than brief degradation. This extends prior byte-level sensitivity analyses from "which bytes inside the container need protection" to "whether the container should be used at all".
>
> **(5) A real-time video testbed and a latency decomposition method.** Existing ISAC platforms provide video only as file streaming with player-side buffering and impose no latency constraint. We build a complete real-time chain — camera capture → H.264 encoding → UDP → OFDM-ISAC → decoding → display — establish an end-to-end latency measurement and decomposition method, and identify a queue-driven positive-feedback loop: a decoder stall propagates back through the receive buffer and demodulator blocking to deepen the frame queue, making latency grow without bound. Reducing four queue depths from 32 to 4–8 frames and replacing blocking enqueue with non-blocking drop lowers the latency drift rate from 【TBD】 to 【TBD】 ms/min, with roughly 80% of the residual attributable to the decoder and only a few milliseconds to the radio link.

## 四、摘要的贡献句（三段式压缩版）

**中文**

> 本文把"外码要多强"的参数问题，转换为"保护该加在哪一层"的架构问题。通过在真实 OFDM-ISAC 链路上对四种分帧方式的对照测量，定位了分帧元数据的单点故障，证明边界失守导致的流级失步不能被纠错强度弥补；并在同一误码流下给出容器与解码器约两个数量级的放大差异。据此提出"边界格子应与传输块对齐"的设计准则。

**English**

> This paper reframes the question from "how strong should the outer code be" to "at which layer should protection be applied". Controlled measurements of four framing variants over a live OFDM-ISAC link localize the framing metadata as a single point of failure, show that stream-level desynchronization caused by boundary loss cannot be compensated by coding strength, and quantify a two-order-of-magnitude amplification spread across container and decoder stacks under an identical error stream. The resulting design principle is that boundary grids should align with transport blocks.

### 5.1 五个"待补"必须在投稿前填上

| 位置 | 需要的数据 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| (2) M1 失步率 vs p | 曲线或表 | D2 实验 |

### 5.2 三个 ⚠️ 需要核实的表述

| 表述 | 风险 | 怎么核实 |
|---|---|---|
| "**首次**在同一误码流下横向对比四种栈" | 若有人做过则翻车 | 投稿前用关键词再检索一轮（ISAC + video + container + comparison） |
| "容器与解码器相差约两个数量级" | 4× 与 500× 是 125 倍，称"两个数量级"需谨慎 | 建议改为"**约两个数量级（125 倍）**"，或直接写倍数 |
| "既有工作只关注包内字节敏感度" | Iqbal 2019 确实如此，但可能有其他工作做了跨容器对比 | 引用 Iqbal 时注明"就我们所检索到的文献" |

### 5.3 三处措辞建议

| 现在 | 建议 |
|---|---|
| "本文**发现**外码的真实作用是……" | 改为"本文**表明**边界保护的缺失是失效主因"（避免"发现"这种叙事化表述） |
| "本文**优化**了队列" | 改为"本文**调整**队列深度并改用非阻塞入队"（"优化"需要对照数据支撑） |
| "**我们的方案**优于 MPEG-TS" | 改为"在同一误码流下，定长分片方案的失步事件为零，而 TS 路径出现持续冻结"（陈述事实，不下优劣判断） |

### 5.4 贡献排序是否需要调整

当前顺序：准则 → 单点故障 → 检出不足 → γ → 试验床

**另一种排法**（把实测最强的放前面）：

1. 单点故障 + 四模式消融（最硬的实验）
2. 准则（由 1 提炼）
3. 检出不足 + 两种方案（由 1 延伸）
4. γ（独立贡献）
5. 试验床（工程贡献）

**判断依据**：如果审稿人偏好"先给证据再给结论"，用第二种；如果偏好"先给框架再给验证"，用第一种。

**我的建议**：期刊论文用第二种（证据优先），学位论文用第一种（框架优先）。
